Motivation

Africa shouldn't be left behind

Most of the AI deployed today is trained on Western data. It can be used by Africans. It doesn't represent or understand them.

The technical divide between the African continent and the rest of the world is widening fast, especially in AI and language technology. But it isn't about talent. Africa has brilliant people, researchers, and builders. The infrastructure is there, or catching up. What's missing is something deeper.

That's the gap I want to help close. Not by deciding from the outside what Africa needs, but by working on tools and research that include African data, languages, and contexts from the ground up. see the projects →
